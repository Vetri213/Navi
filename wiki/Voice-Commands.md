# Voice Commands Reference

Complete guide to interacting with Navi using voice commands.

---

##  Wake Word

### Activating Navi

**Command:** *"Hey Navi"*

- Activates the assistant from anywhere
- Works even when panel is minimized
- No need to click anything
- Always listening in the background

**Alternatives:**
- Click the purple "ASK NAVI" button
- Use keyboard shortcut (if configured)

---

##  Natural Language Commands

Navi understands natural language! You don't need to memorize specific commands. Just speak naturally.

### General Format

```
"Help me [action]"
"How do I [action]"
"Show me how to [action]"
"I want to [action]"
"Can you help me [action]"
```

##  Tips for Better Voice Recognition

### DO:

 **Speak clearly** - Enunciate your words
 **Use natural language** - Talk like you normally would
 **Be specific** - *"Open Chrome"* vs *"Open browser"*
 **Wait for response** - Let Navi finish speaking
 **Rephrase if needed** - Try different words

### DON'T:

 **Shout** - Normal speaking voice works best
 **Whisper** - Navi might not hear you
 **Speak too fast** - Take your time
 **Use technical jargon** - Keep it simple
 **Interrupt** - Wait for Navi to finish

##  Context-Aware Responses

Navi adapts its questions based on what it's asking you to do:

### Visual Tasks
*"Do you see it?"*
- When pointing to something on screen
- When describing a visual element

### Action Tasks
*"Did that work?"*
- After clicking something
- After opening an application

### Input Tasks
*"Did you finish typing that?"*
- After asking you to type
- After entering information

##  Best Practices

1. **Start simple** - Begin with basic commands
2. **Be patient** - AI processing takes a few seconds
3. **Provide context** - *"Help me print this document"* vs *"Print"*
4. **Use follow-ups** - Build on previous commands
5. **Ask for help** - Say "I don't understand" if confused

---

##  When Commands Don't Work

### If Navi doesn't understand:

1. **Rephrase your request**
   - Instead of: *"Locate dining establishment"*
   - Try: *"Find a restaurant near me"*

2. **Be more specific**
   - Instead of: *"Open program"*
   - Try: *"Open Microsoft Word"*

3. **Break it down**
   - Instead of: *"Help me do everything"*
   - Try: *"Help me open Chrome"* (then continue)

4. **Check your microphone**
   - Ensure it's working
   - Adjust volume if needed
